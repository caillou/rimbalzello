# loop strip

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

The loop strip on the phone play screen, driving the looper. Four boxed zones `1/4 1/8 1/16 1/32`, longest on the left, hairline borders. Touching a zone engages a loop; the strip tracks the finger across zones while held (slide changes the size); sliding off the strip or lifting is a release. The held zone is white with black text. `touch-action: none`.

The step display highlights the played step and, while a loop is held, also shows the playhead step dimmed.

## User stories covered

35, 36, 37, 38

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Playwright with synthetic pointers: press on `1/8` while playing, slide to `1/32`, slide off the strip; the held zone follows the finger and the loop releases on slide-off (observed through the transport's played step or the step display).
- [ ] Playwright: pressing a zone while stopped does nothing.
- [ ] Layout rule checks still pass at the phone viewport.
