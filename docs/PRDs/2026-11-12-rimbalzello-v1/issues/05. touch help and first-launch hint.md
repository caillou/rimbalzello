# touch help and first-launch hint

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

On touch screens, `// help` toggles a hint box over the lower part of the pad (`designs/v2-06-phone-help.png`): the gestures including the two-finger undo, plus a line each on anchor, loop, mutes (tap the step display labels) and sound. `// help` turns white while open; tapping it again closes the box. The pad stays playable under and around the box.

On first launch, a one-line hint inside the pad reads `tap · drag · two-finger tap undo · double-tap+slide fine`. It goes away after the first pad move and does not come back on that device (localStorage, try/catch).

Laptop behaviour of `// help` (keycap hints) is the keyboard play issue.
