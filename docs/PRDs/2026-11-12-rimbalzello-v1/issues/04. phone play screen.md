# phone play screen

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

The phone layout of the play screen, wired end to end to the setup store, transport and kit, so a player on a phone can play the map. See `design.md` (Play screen, phone portrait; Pad interaction; Mutes; Input rules) and `designs/v2-01-phone.png`.

- **Header**: `# rimbalzello`, `− 128 bpm +` (− and + step by 1; the panel and hold-repeat come with the tempo panel issue), `stop` / `play`.
- **Pad**: canvas or direct DOM writes outside Solid's update cycle. Crosshair, faint ring, yellow square, X/Y readout 0–100. Keeps clear of the left 16 px. Pointer events go through the pad gesture interpreter; its intents drive the transport's glide and the setup store's pad undo. `▶ tap to play` overlay stays from the tracer.
- **Step display**: three 32-character lines, `x` in the drum colour for hits, `·` grey for rests, played step highlighted yellow with black text, updated from the transport outside Solid's update cycle. The drum labels are the mute toggles (real buttons, at least 44 px hit area); a muted line goes grey and its label struck through.
- **Strips**: bd, sd, hh densities, chaos, drift, drive. Sliders move relative to the touch (no tap-jump), display 0–100, store bytes. Real labelled inputs with visible focus.
- **Bottom bar and footer**: render `→ sound`, `set anchor`, `↺ return on bar` and `// help  // about  // source` in place (later issues give them behaviour; `// source` links to the repo now).
- Input rules: `touch-action: none` on pad and strips; no text selection, callouts or context menus. The screen fills `100dvh` and never scrolls.
- Playwright layout rule helper, reused by later layout issues: at a viewport, the play screen does not scroll, every control is at least 44 px, no two controls overlap. Apply it at the phone viewport (393×659). Phone breakpoint values go in tunables.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Layout rule checks pass at 393×659 with the default setup and with the step display at its widest content.
- [ ] Playwright: tapping a step display drum label toggles its mute (accessible state changes, label struck through); strip labels do not mute.
- [ ] Playwright: dragging a strip moves its value relative to the drag; a tap on the track does not change the value.
- [ ] Playwright: a tap on the pad changes the X/Y readout toward the tap point; a two-finger tap (two synthetic pointers) restores the previous readout.
- [ ] Every control is a button or labelled input reachable by Tab with a visible focus ring.
