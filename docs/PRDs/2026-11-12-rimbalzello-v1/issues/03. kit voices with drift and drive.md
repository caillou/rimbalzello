# kit voices with drift and drive

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

The real native Web Audio kit behind the kit interface the tracer pinned.

- **bd**: pitched oscillator or high-Q filter ping with a pitch envelope and punch. Parameters freq, punch, tone, decay, level.
- **sd**: tuned resonators plus filtered noise. Parameters freq, tone, snappy, decay, level.
- **hh**: six detuned square oscillators through band-pass and high-pass, 808-style. Parameter level.
- Parameters are bytes 0–255 mapped to musical ranges; Peaks' parameter names and voice structure are the starting point (`handover-grids-peaks.md`). Mappings live in the tunables module.
- **Drift**: one 0–255 amount scaling per-hit random offsets in pitch (cents), decay, level, timing (1–2 ms) and filter cutoff. Every hit draws its own offsets. Per-dimension maximums in tunables.
- **Accent** drives velocity.
- **Mutes**: per-drum gains on the bus, acting at once.
- **Drive**: all drums sum into a `WaveShaperNode` with a soft-clip curve, 4× oversampling and gain compensation so turning drive up does not make the mix much louder. Curve and compensation in tunables.
- Cancel: hits scheduled but not started can be cancelled.

The by-ear tuning is a separate human issue; here the goal is correct structure and sensible starting values.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `OfflineAudioContext` smoke tests: each drum renders non-silent output at default sound; the summed kit at drive 0 and drive 255 does not clip; hits start at their scheduled times (within the drift timing bound when drift > 0, exact when drift = 0).
- [ ] Test: RMS of a one-bar render at drive 255 is within a tunable factor (start at +3 dB) of the render at drive 0.
- [ ] Test: with drift 0 two renders of the same hit are identical; with drift 255 they differ.
- [ ] Test: a muted drum contributes silence from the mute time onward; a cancelled hit does not sound.

## Notes from earlier reviews

- From the tap-to-play review: the pinned kit interface (`trigger(drum, time, velocity, sound)`, `cancelScheduled()`, `setMuted()`) has no way yet to pass drift or drive; add it here (e.g. `setDrive(byte)`, drift as a trigger argument or setter). Nothing tests cancellation yet: a no-op `cancelScheduled` still passes, so the "a cancelled hit does not sound" criterion needs a real test.
