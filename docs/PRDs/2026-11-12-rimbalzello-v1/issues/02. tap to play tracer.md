# tap to play tracer

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

The thinnest end-to-end path a player can hear on the deployed site: open the app, see `▶ tap to play` over a dark pad, tap once, hear a fixed groove at 128 bpm through the native kit; `stop` and `play` in the header stop and restart it from step 0.

- The groove is a fixed 32-step pattern for bd, sd and hh (one bar of 32nd-note steps), hard-coded from one Grids map node at fixed densities. The pattern generator is built in parallel and replaces it later.
- The kit interface is the one from the PRD and is pinned here so the kit and transport issues build on it: trigger a drum at a time with a velocity and its sound parameters, cancel hits that are scheduled but not started, set per-drum mute gains. Behind it, minimal native Web Audio voices for bd, sd and hh (simple is fine; the kit issue does the real voices) summing into a master gain.
- A minimal scheduler reads `AudioContext.currentTime` and schedules hits a lookahead window ahead (lookahead from the tunables module). The transport issue replaces its internals with the pure, clock-injected transport; keep the boundary between "what to play at time t" and "schedule it on the AudioContext" clean so that swap is local.
- The AudioContext is created and resumed in the `▶ tap to play` gesture. No iOS silent-switch or wake-lock handling yet (audio session issue).
- Styling only as far as the screen reads as the app: dark background, Plex Mono, `# rimbalzello` header, yellow `▶ tap to play` block (`v2-05-phone-tap-to-play.png`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the deployed site, one tap on `▶ tap to play` starts audible playback in desktop Chrome, Safari and Firefox; the overlay disappears.
- [ ] `stop` silences playback (scheduled-but-unstarted hits are cancelled); `play` restarts at step 0.
- [ ] An `OfflineAudioContext` render of one bar through the kit interface is not silent, does not clip, and has onsets at the expected bd step times (tolerance within one render quantum).
- [ ] The engine side (pattern data, scheduler logic) imports no Solid code.
