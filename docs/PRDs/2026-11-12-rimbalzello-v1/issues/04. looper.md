# looper

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

The looper inside the transport, implementing the PRD's Looper rules 1–20 as written. In short: zones 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 = 8, 4, 2, 1 steps. Engaging takes the start `s` from the step the player hears at that moment (output latency included, not the step being scheduled ahead), not snapped to the loop length; it takes effect from the next step boundary. While held, the playhead keeps running and the played step is (s + ((p − s) mod L)) mod 32. Sliding to another size keeps `s` and the phase. Release slips back to the playhead step. Engaging, sliding and releasing cancel hits scheduled but not started. Only while playing; stop ends the loop; a zone held at play does not engage. Chaos re-rolls on the playhead's step 0, never on a loop wrap. Swing follows the playhead slot. A pending return still lands on the playhead's step 0 and the loop reads the restored setup. Pattern changes, mutes, sound, drift and drive stay live inside the loop.

The transport exposes the played step and the playhead step for the step display. Points the rules leave open: take the simplest reading and note it in a comment.

Interface for callers: engage/change size (zone), release, and an "audible step now" input from the real-time wrapper (or the transport computes it from clock and output latency).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tests with a fake clock for the PRD's statements: holding 1/4 from step 4 then sliding to 1/8 when the playhead is at step 10 plays 6, 7, 4, 5, 6, …; holding 1/4 from step 4 then sliding to 1/32 repeats step 4; a 1/8 loop started at step 30 plays 30, 31, 0, 1, and holding it across the bar line does not re-roll chaos while the playhead crossing step 0 does.
- [ ] Test: 1/8 touched while step 13 is heard plays 13, 14, 15, 16, 13, … and on release plays the step the playhead has reached.
- [ ] Test: the start is the heard step, not the step currently being scheduled (with a lookahead of several steps).
- [ ] Test: a pending return lands on the playhead's step 0 during a held loop, and the next looped step reads the restored setup.
- [ ] Tests: engage while stopped does nothing; stop ends a held loop; hits scheduled beyond the next boundary are cancelled on engage and release.
- [ ] Test: an accented looped step stays accented on every 1/32 retrigger.
