# acceptance on the target devices

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

Pierre plays the deployed app at `rimbalzello.caillou.ch` on his iPhone (Safari) and laptop (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) and records what passes and what needs follow-up issues. Gesture thresholds and layout breakpoints get their final values from these sessions, in the tunables module. The DNS record and Pages custom domain are confirmed working.

## User stories covered

4, 5, 6, 7, 14, 15, 16, 17, 19, 50, 51, 52

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] iPhone: sound with the silent switch on; music resumes (or shows `tap to resume`) after a call and an app switch; the screen stays on while playing.
- [ ] iPhone: hits feel tight; tap, drag, two-finger undo and double-tap-and-slide feel right; the iOS edge swipe does not navigate back during play.
- [ ] iPhone: layout breakpoints measured on Pierre's devices are set in tunables; the play screen does not scroll at any of them.
- [ ] Laptop: keyboard play works in Chrome, Safari and Firefox, including on Pierre's Swiss layout.
- [ ] A shared link opened on the other device plays the same groove.
- [ ] `https://rimbalzello.caillou.ch` serves the app, `/about/` and the 404 page.
- [ ] Each failure becomes a follow-up issue file in this folder.
