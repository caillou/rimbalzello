# short and compact layouts

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

The phone screen fits shorter viewports without scrolling.

- **Short layout**: the six strips fold into `mix` (bd sd hh) and `fx` (chaos drift drive) tabs (`designs/v2-01-phone.png` shows them). The pad keeps at least the tunable minimum height (about 240 px). Loop strip, bottom bar and footer stay visible.
- **Compact layout** (iPhone SE in Safari, about 375×553): slimmer header, tighter rows, and the step display moves into the pad as an overlay (`designs/v2-02-phone-compact.png`). Mute labels keep their 44 px hit area.
- Sound mode, tempo panel and touch help are laid out for both. Exact compact shapes beyond the screenshot are the agent's call within `design.md`'s visual rules.
- Breakpoints are viewport heights (`100dvh`) in the tunables module; starting points from `design.md`.

## User stories covered

51, 52, 55

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Layout rule checks pass at a short phone viewport (for example 393×600) and at 375×553, in play mode (both tabs), sound mode (each drum tab), with the tempo panel open and with help open.
- [ ] At both viewports the pad is at least the tunable minimum height, and the loop strip, bottom bar and footer are visible without scrolling.
- [ ] At the phone viewport (393×659) the layout is the short layout, with the strips in `mix` and `fx` tabs (`designs/v2-01-phone.png`).

## Notes from earlier reviews

- 393×659 gets the short layout (mix/fx tabs, as v2-01-phone.png shows): with six strips the pad is 171 px, about 127 px once the loop strip lands, and a 240 px pad needs a viewport of about 772 px. This replaces the criterion that 393×659 stays the six-strip layout. See https://github.com/caillou/rimbalzello/issues/2.
