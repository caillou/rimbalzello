# tempo panel

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

Tapping the bpm readout replaces the strips with `## tempo`: bpm with − and +, swing with − and +, and a `tap tempo` button (`designs/v2-04-phone-tempo.png`; where the screenshot shows sliders, the PRD's − and + win, a slider may sit between them). − and + repeat while held, here and in the header (repeat delay and rate in tunables). Tempo 60–200 whole bpm; swing 50–75 % in 1 % steps. Tap tempo averages the last few taps (count in tunables), rounds and clamps. Tapping the readout again or `← play` in the bottom bar closes the panel; while it is open the bottom bar's first item is `← play`.

## User stories covered

8, 9, 10, 11, 12

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Unit test for tap tempo: taps 500 ms apart give 120 bpm; taps faster than 200 bpm clamp to 200; an outlier gap starts a new average (or the simplest reading, documented).
- [ ] Playwright: holding + for a tunable duration raises bpm by more than one step; releasing stops it; bpm never exceeds 200.
- [ ] Playwright: swing − at 50 % stays at 50 %; + at 75 % stays at 75 %.
- [ ] Layout rule checks pass at the phone viewport with the panel open.
