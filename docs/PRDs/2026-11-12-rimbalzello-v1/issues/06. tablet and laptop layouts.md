# tablet and laptop layouts

## PRD

[2026-11-12-rimbalzello-v1](../prd.md)

## What to build

Wide viewports get two columns; landscape phones get the rotate hint.

- **Laptop** (`designs/07-laptop-shortcuts-hidden.png` for the look only; it is outdated for behaviour): pad on the left, full height, with the 5×5 map nodes as faint `+` marks at 0, 64, 128, 192, 256 (outer nodes on the pad edges, unlike the screenshot) and the X/Y readout. Right column: step display; strips in two columns (bd / chaos, sd / drift, hh / drive) with swing inline; loop strip; a hairline; `## bd`, `## sd`, `## hh` sound sections side by side. No tabs. Bottom row: footer bottom left, `set anchor` and `↺ return on bar` bottom right. The tempo panel on the laptop only adds tap tempo.
- **Tablet** (about 700 px wide or more): portrait per `designs/v2-07-tablet.png`, pad on top, below it step display and all six strips on the left, loop strip and sound drum tabs on the right; landscape like the laptop. Drum tabs are the only tabs.
- **Rotate hint**: a viewport wider than tall and too short for the tablet layout shows a hint to turn the phone upright.
- Breakpoints in the tunables module.
